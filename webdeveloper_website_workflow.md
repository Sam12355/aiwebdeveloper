# Webdeveloper.lk Website Workflow

**From customer lead to live website in 3 days**

## Customer Journey

### 1. Home Page

**Question:** What is your business?

### 2. Business Type

Enter business name, contact details, logo.

### 3. Business Details

Choose pages, business type, website path.

### 4. Theme Selection

Choose a website template.

### 5. Invoice & Advance Payment

Bank slip upload or card payment.

### 6. Payment Success

Customer account created.

### 7. Customer Dashboard

Track project progress.

### 8. Demo Review

Review homepage / demo.

### 9. Final Review

Approve final website.

### 10. Final Payment

Pay balance payment.

### 11. Website Live

Launch complete.

## Admin Panel Workflow

### A1. Pending Lead

Admin checks the new customer lead.

### A2. Verify Payment

Admin verifies the advance payment.

### A3. Create Project

Admin creates the project after payment verification.

### A4. Assign Staff

Admin assigns staff members to the project.

### A5. Update Project Stage

Admin updates the project progress stage.

### A6. Send Notifications

Admin sends customer notifications.

### A7. Upload Demo

Admin uploads the demo website for review.

### A8. Approve Launch

Admin approves the final launch after customer approval and final payment.
